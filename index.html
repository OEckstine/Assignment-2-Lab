Follow the Money

The 2024 Senate Races

By: Olivia Eckstine

The 25 top-fundraising Senate candidates of 2024 raised more than $1.1 billion combined, with Democrats bringing in about $784 million, more than double the roughly $341 million raised by Republicans. Click either party's bar to see its candidates ranked, and use the buttons above the chart to switch between total funds and each source of money: individual donors, PACs, party committees, and candidates' own wealth. The breakdown shows that money did not guarantee a win. Sherrod Brown, Colin Allred, and Jon Tester were the race's three biggest fundraisers, and all three lost. It also shows how far personal fortunes can go: David Trone spent $62.9 million of his own money, nearly all of the Democrats' self-funding in this group, and still lost his primary, while Republicans Rick Scott and Eric Hovde each put in more than $20 million of their own. Comparing sources reveals which candidates were powered by grassroots donors and which leaned on PACs or their own bank accounts.

Source: Federal Election Commission, 2023-2024 candidate summary data.
